Welcome to Assam

The Land of the Red River and Blue Hills

Assam is a northeastern Indian state known for the Brahmaputra Valley, tea plantations, biodiversity, vibrant traditions and diverse communities.

Whether you love adventure, history, spirituality, wildlife or food, Assam offers many ways to explore at your own pace.

  • Capital: Dispur
  • Major city and gateway: Guwahati
  • Major river: Brahmaputra
  • Principal language: Assamese, alongside many other languages
  • Famous products: Assam tea, Muga silk and traditional crafts

Traditions & experiences

Experience the Culture of Assam

Discover local traditions, delicious food, celebrations and handicrafts that reflect Assam's cultural richness.

🎉

Bihu Festival

Rongali, Kongali and Bhogali Bihu mark different stages of the agricultural calendar through dance, music and community celebrations.

🍛

Assamese Cuisine

Try rice, khar, tenga, pitika, seasonal vegetables and pitha. Food traditions vary across communities and regions.

🧵

Muga & Eri Silk

Learn about Assam's silk heritage and traditional weaving. Look for genuine local products from responsible sellers.

🍃

Tea Heritage

Visit permitted tea estates, learn about tea production and enjoy Assam's celebrated tea culture.

🎭

Sattriya & Satra Traditions

Explore classical Sattriya dance, devotional music, theatre and the cultural institutions of the Vaishnavite tradition.

🦏

Wildlife & Nature

Experience guided wildlife tourism while respecting protected areas, local communities and animal habitats.

Essential Assam Travel Guide

Helpful information to prepare for a smooth and enjoyable journey.

Best Time to Visit

October–April: Often comfortable for sightseeing and many outdoor activities.

May–September: Rainy conditions may affect roads, river travel and national park access.

Safari seasons and opening dates vary. Check official notices before booking.

How to Reach Assam

By air: Guwahati's Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport is a major gateway.

By train: Guwahati and other towns have railway connections. Check schedules for your route.

By road: Buses, taxis and private vehicles connect many destinations; travel time varies.

Local Transport

Use taxis, buses, app-based transport where available, and local shared vehicles. Availability varies by location.

For Majuli and other river destinations, check ferry schedules, weather conditions and the last return service.

Travel Tips

Carry identification, booking confirmations, suitable footwear and rain protection when needed.

Respect temple customs, ask before photographing people, avoid littering and follow wildlife safety instructions.

During monsoon or floods, check local advisories before travelling.

Food to Try

Look for khar, masor tenga, aloo pitika, rice dishes, pitha, laru and Assam tea.

Ask about ingredients and preparation if you have allergies or dietary restrictions.
